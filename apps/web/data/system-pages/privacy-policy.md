# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

## 1. Overview

SoulSync Global processes personal data to operate a faith-aware matrimony platform with strong privacy defaults. This policy describes what we collect, why, and your choices.

## 2. Data we collect

### 2.1 Account data

Email, username, authentication artifacts, theme preferences, and security logs.

### 2.2 Biodata and preferences

Profile fields you choose to complete, partner preferences, visibility settings, gallery media, and community participation.

### 2.3 Sensitive categories

Some biodata (health, family, guardian contact, marriage records) is especially sensitive. We encrypt selected fields at the application layer and restrict access by role and visibility rules.

## 3. How we use data

- Provide search, compatibility, and biodata viewing according to your visibility choices
- Operate communities, notifications, and marriage confirmation workflows
- Secure the service, prevent abuse, and comply with law

We do not sell personal data.

## 4. Sharing

We share data only as needed: infrastructure providers under contract, community moderators within their scope, or when you explicitly request contact (e.g., guardian flows). Public biodata respects section-level visibility.

## 5. Retention

We retain data while your account is active and as required for legal, security, or dispute purposes. You may request deletion subject to exceptions (e.g., confirmed audit records).

## 6. Your rights

Depending on jurisdiction, you may access, correct, export, or delete personal data. Contact **hello@soulsync.global**.

## 7. International transfers

Where data crosses borders, we use appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable law.

## 8. Updates

We will post policy updates on this page with a revised date.
