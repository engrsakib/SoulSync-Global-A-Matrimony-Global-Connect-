# Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to paid features and **credit wallet** purchases on SoulSync Global where a monetary charge is processed through our payment partners.

## 2. Digital credits

Credits purchased for in-platform use (for example profile boosts or premium actions) are generally **non-refundable** once credited to your wallet, except where required by law or as stated at checkout.

## 3. Erroneous charges

If you believe you were charged in error (duplicate charge, wrong amount, or charge after a confirmed cancellation), contact **hello@soulsync.global** within **30 days** with your account email and transaction reference. We will review and refund eligible cases to the original payment method when possible.

## 4. Chargebacks

Unauthorized chargebacks without contacting support first may result in account suspension while we investigate. We prefer to resolve billing issues directly with members.

## 5. Regional rights

Where local consumer law grants mandatory cooling-off or refund rights, those rights apply in addition to this policy.

## 6. Contact

Billing questions: **hello@soulsync.global**
